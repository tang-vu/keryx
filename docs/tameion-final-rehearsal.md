**Five judge answers**

- **What is innovative?** The two-toll design separates buying access from rewarding eligible creators whose evidence is actually cited. Decisions, excerpts and receipts make the research economics inspectable. Missing-evidence procurement is a portable prototype; funded provider execution remains a next stage.
- **How autonomous is it?** Within an admitted question and budget, the agent discovers, selects, reads and synthesizes with visible decisions. Operator adds obligation/liquidity checks and recovery. This is neither a contract-enforced general policy wallet nor a continuously unattended operator; paid fulfillment is not guaranteed.
- **Do people return?** The owner confirmed 0xacee’s October 15 VoiceReady task after the October 12 storage/backup session. It used a zero-USDC source cap and sponsored model/search usage, with no payment rows. That establishes documented return use, not paid repeat demand.
- **What proves payment?** The separate walkthrough records a mainnet reward. October 16 checks matched its digest, Circle’s completed transfer and V1 payout registration; they do not establish individual onchain finality or factual correctness. Hoàng’s sponsored testnet payment is separate evidence, never mainnet revenue or customer-funded demand.
- **Was it useful?** MDN passages informed VoiceReady’s preview-lifecycle review. An assistant separately implemented and checked changes; Keryx did not validate the audio. FFmpeg research failed, and finished-customer use and independently accepted complete paid fulfillment remain unconfirmed.
