# Product clarity, pricing discovery and support

The owner requested one coherent update based on an outside website critique.
Live homepage inspection on October 12 found an existing research composer,
sponsored trial, paid research entry, source browsing and payment proof. The
archive-only diagnosis and supplied numeric score are not established facts.
Useful findings: audiences/outcomes need a clearer introduction, costs/support
need discoverable pages, and saved reports need product context.

## Behavior

- Home leads with cited AI research and visible source decisions. The question
  form remains the primary action; explanatory sections follow it. Researcher,
  builder/writer and agent-developer paths point to existing surfaces.
  On narrow screens the guide has its own reserved row and the decorative globe
  yields space to readable product text; existing research-workspace decoration
  is unchanged. First-viewport question/action and non-overlap gates still apply.
- Indexed `/pricing` uses `quoteA2aResearch` and deployment configuration for
  six-decimal Quick/Deep examples. A quote does not establish availability. The
  buyer workspace checks live readiness and remains intentionally noindex.
  Browser caps, sponsored trials, prepaid reserves/fees and manual Monthly
  requests have separate terms. Unused prepaid reserves stay in the package.
- `/about` explains the open-source project, evidence limits and support.
  **support@keryx.cc** is owner-confirmed October 12 and used in the footer,
  policy pages and machine-readable description. No endorsements, team
  credentials or security certification are invented.
- Public archive indexes, topic collections and public report permalinks carry
  neutral product context. Private/unlisted reports retain their boundaries.
  Original evidence/payment labels and historical context remain intact.
- Metadata/social artwork explain the research outcome. Remove the universal
  zero-USD offer. New pages have their own canonical and existing English/default
  alternates and sitemap entries. `llms.txt` replaces obsolete testnet, fixed-fee
  and overbroad payment claims.
- Existing coarse telemetry moves to a client component with the same tab/day
  key, event, omitted credentials and failure handling. Product prose renders
  on the server. English remains the only shipped UI locale.

credential-free offline testnet fixture: crawlable text, own canonicals/indexing,
configured quote amounts, sitemap/`llms.txt`, support, keyboard FAQ and navigation
at 320/390/1024/1366 pixels. Browser APIs are intercepted; research/payment requests
fail the check. Optional screenshots use `KERYX_PRODUCT_SCREENSHOT_DIR`. CI runs
the fixture after build. Original chat/mobile-header fixtures cover existing
questions, follow-ups, refusal/recovery and wallet/menu interaction.

inspection and independent source review apply. Actual hosted aggregate/domain/
