# Research value and free evidence — October 16, 2026

The owner's deeper product pass found two related orchestration defects: initial
free/cache deliveries bypassed interim sufficiency, and gap expansion priced a
fresh cached article as another paid read. Enough free evidence could therefore
be followed by an unnecessary toll, while useful cached evidence could be
excluded after the fetch allocation was spent.

## Delivery and safety behavior

Assess delivered initial evidence before continuing to another source. Reuse the
existing sufficiency stop, unread-decision correction and query-local reservation
release. An unavailable assessment stops further purchases while retaining reads
and receipts. Pending and settled-undelivered amounts keep their existing holds.
An initial cache whose content disappeared is an unread selection, not delivered
evidence; it cannot satisfy the stop condition or authorize an unreserved toll.

Offer a fresh cached gap candidate at zero access cost. Before delivery, confirm
that its cached content still exists; a disappeared cache must satisfy the normal
paid budget check. Source terms, rights, recipient exclusions and human review
remain required. This changes no grant, custody, settlement or reward authority.

The shared server orchestrator serves web, API, CLI, remote/stdio MCP and bot
research, including hosted research opened from desktop and extensions. No client
schema, package capability or native reader contract changes. Source acceptance
does not establish deployment or publication of any surface.

Regression coverage includes free/cache stopping, unread reservations, unavailable
assessment, pending and settled-undelivered holds, expired/missing/empty/throwing
cache, exhausted-budget expansion, changed terms/rights and valid checkpoint
replay. A separate final-only assessment failure follows an additional read;
its receipts remain while citation rewards fail closed. The controlled 48-trial
study must be reproduced for the changed source, without outbound calls.

purchased evidence inspectable in that same job. Separate payment receipts,
offline policy comparisons and system-reported coverage do not supply it.
