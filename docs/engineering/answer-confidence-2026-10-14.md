# Confidence in delivered research summaries

The owner reported that current answers all show Low and requested an improvement
on October 14. The orchestrator computed an evidence verdict with High, Moderate
and Low outcomes, but ordinary answer delivery replaced every outcome with Low,
including separately reviewed sentence-to-excerpt summaries.

Ordinary public summaries now assess the reviewed sentences actually delivered.
Every requested target must have a sentence admitted against the final evidence
ledger, coverage at least 0.4 and a sufficient final assessment. One supported
publisher group can yield Moderate. High additionally requires coverage at least
0.7 and paired evidence with support at least 0.7 from two distinct publisher/work
groups for **every** target. Existing shared-DOI, identical-body and publisher
domain grouping prevents observed duplicates from counting as corroboration.
These groups do not prove independent authorship or factual correctness.

An excerpt without an admitted sentence cannot fill a summary target or count as
summary corroboration. Missing targets, an incomplete/failed final assessment,
unresolved disagreement and delivered synthetic demo statements remain Low.
The existing final-run synthetic demotion also includes skipped synthetic
decisions and rejected recorded evidence; the streamed verdict preserves that
same restriction so it cannot announce a higher level than the completed run.
Explained source preferences cap confidence at Moderate. Excerpt-only output and
unavailable synthesis stay Low. Retained private/package originals and the
separately gated decision-brief format retain their prior confidence contract.
Recorded historical runs are not relabeled or migrated.

Confidence expresses bounded support for the delivered summary. The notice still
states that independent factual correctness and complete synthesis are unverified;
this change does not accept the broader decision/checklist completeness gate.
It adds no model/search/read calls and changes no evidence reward eligibility,
signer authority, cap, payment reconciliation or source recipient rule.

Web/API/SSE, saved reports, receipts and remote MCP expose the recorded verdict.
OpenAI-compatible and A2A responses retain the same answer/evidence plus their
dispatch/receipt links; no new confidence field is added to those schemas.
Word-budget and exact-sentence renderers accept every confidence level while
retaining their existing binding, count and operational-notice checks.
Desktop, buyer CLI, stdio MCP, extension and bot clients
retain their existing server/display/handoff roles and contracts. Focused tests
exercise completed public Moderate/High results through the orchestrator, saved
receipt and shared adapters, together with Low failure paths and preserved private
originals. Synthetic regressions verify classification and plumbing, not a measured
real-world answer-accuracy improvement. No new paid evaluation allowance is used.

Merge after required CI and independent review. The existing ROOT release owner
accumulates this runtime change into its next deployment batch; deployed and
published surface identities must be verified before claiming live delivery.
