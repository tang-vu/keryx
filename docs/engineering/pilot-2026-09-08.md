# Owner-operated Engineering pilot, September 8, 2026

This Arc-testnet purchase used the registered first-party Engineering source. It
demonstrates paid delivery and creator accounting, not external customer adoption.
The underlying documentation is also publicly available in this repository.

Question: "How does the Keryx buyer journal a job before submission, and how can it
recover after losing the response without paying again? Use the Keryx Engineering
source for the documented behavior."

The Quick job completed in 36.855 seconds. The package cost was 0.05 USDC:
0.02 service fee and 0.03 creator reserve. Creator spending was 0.017 USDC;
the remaining 0.013 reserve is not an automatic refund. Buyer payment status was
seller-reported settled, not an independently verified inbound Circle payment.

## Evidence and quality

Both substantive targets (journal before submission; recover without paying again)
reached coverage 1. The planner added a redundant third target about documented
behavior, which remained at 0. Reported grounded claim rate was 0.666667 and the
answer retained its low-confidence warning. This is a partial research-quality
result; the redundant target remains an improvement to make.

## Receipt regression and recovery

The initial portable receipt contained only the citation payment, even though job
accounting included both legs. The endpoint used a citation-only database query.
Version 0.22.10 (`438301e`) switches it to the complete creator-attempt query.
No database rewrite or payment resend was required.

After production deployment, GET-only buyer resume on the same job verified request
binding, digest integrity and a complete two-payment ledger: 0.002 access plus
0.015 citation, with zero pending or simulated creator amounts. Both immutable
receipt snapshots remain in the private local journal:

- Before: `sha256:12aaf2f10449b8728e9f1c9b8a49175521f969855c3da4b706e0864b0fdfb6ec`
- After: `sha256:c07503a70c7fe18d42d7650ec2a671a6beed1931c90baa8b3ee3fdfc72158ccb`

Validation: 31 focused receipt/buyer tests, TypeScript, CI and production build
passed. Public `/api/health` returned `operational` at commit `438301e`.

## Earlier submission uncertainty

An earlier attempt with the original buyer returned HTTP 402; server logs reported
`insufficient_balance`, with no payment acknowledgement or retrievable job. Its
authorization was still live, so its journal remains unconfirmed and was not replayed.
That signer's Gateway balance was not replenished. Instead, the original buyer's
testnet wallet funded exactly 0.05 USDC for an isolated signer through `depositFor`:
[deposit transaction](https://testnet.arcscan.app/tx/0x7eedccce680e33d2bf7a66e07b4934a23b1329e8718c4a1feabf9404e268ca95).

Private job identifiers, authorization payloads and keys are excluded from this record.
