# Bounded research planning and heading recall

Application 0.26.17 addresses the planning boundary in [#169](https://github.com/tang-vu/keryx/issues/169)
and a bounded context-selection gap relevant to [#128](https://github.com/tang-vu/keryx/issues/128).
The ordinary-browser round on 0.26.16 remains **0/3 useful**: Next.js read its original
without producing a useful checklist, while systemd and SQLite stopped before discovery
with plans exceeding eight targets. Raw planning output and the full Next.js extraction/model
input were not retained. These changes do not establish those historical causes or supersede
the grading. The three-question live scope is closed.

## Planning behavior

The shared prompt distinguishes comparison subjects from complementary source references.
It carries source/version/condition restrictions into substantive targets and keeps presentation
instructions separate. Synthetic Vietnamese comparisons preserve seven independent systemd
targets (three settings' behavior and risks, plus shared prechecks) and seven SQLite targets
(two methods' consistency, concurrent writes and locking, plus restore verification).
Comparing two specific papers on three dimensions still requires six independently inspectable
targets, each retaining the exact paper/version. These examples are offline structures, not
vendor advice or a semantic completeness guarantee for arbitrary generated plans.

The eight-target limit remains. Validation removes only exact trimmed duplicates; malformed
entries cannot be filtered away, merged into an umbrella question or replaced with the original
question. A malformed, overexpanded, truncated or explicitly incomplete plan produces a terminal
request-local refusal. Returned provider counters and the original call outcome remain in memory;
JSON parsing failures retain their existing failed-call outcome even when usage is billable.
No alternate paid planning call follows this outcome. Existing transport fallback, prior circuit
failures and bounded half-open leases retain their contracts.

Original-caller projections offer at most three short excerpts from the request, redact URL
selectors and explain retaining original source/version restrictions when narrowing focus.
They are choices of focus, not complete replacement questions or automatically submitted retries.
The generic error message and normal Error inspection/JSON serialization exclude the private
excerpts. Streaming callers receive the existing terminal error channel; OpenAI non-streaming
callers receive HTTP 422 with `research_plan_refinement_required`. No successful report,
dispatch receipt or settlement is fabricated for the refusal. A planning refusal can still have
incurred compute cost; failed allowance holds remain consumed.

## Context selection

After an actual bounded document read, a supplied HTTPS fragment may prioritize a unique short
standalone extracted line with a matching normalized heading. Retain its contiguous following
text before lexical ranking, including neighboring qualifications where the existing bounds fit.
At most four hints are used after scanning at most sixteen URLs. Unsafe, unmatched, ambiguous
or unsupported fragments retain the original ranking; a partial source scan cannot establish
heading uniqueness. A fragment is a text hint, not verified DOM-anchor identity or complete
section extraction. Read scope remains `bounded-whole-document`.

Keep the 200,000-character scan, 2,000-character source-context union, nine-window maximum,
600-character window and existing combined nomination/candidate ceilings. Source bytes and
UTF-16 offsets remain exact through quote selection and review. Literal quote, source/version
identity, support and creator-reward gates are unchanged. Synthetic inert HTML extraction
with English evidence and Vietnamese targets verifies bounded late-heading recall and exact
review provenance. It does not replay the missing historical Next.js pipeline.
