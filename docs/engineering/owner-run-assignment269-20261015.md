answer. Consent invalidation and affected associations terminalize together; expiry is

reply follows its asynchronous work; there is no authority cache or read-side mutation.

Eligibility retains the exact stored original digest/date, selected identity, independently
retained network evidence, public/unlisted non-erased state, and absence of conflicting
