## Authority and accounting

`~/.local/share/keryx-business-canary-continuation` on the original host. Keep the
new protected authorization and captured official tariff outside that directory.
The grant binds its explicit owner receipt and at most 24-hour deadline, exact
clean deployed commit/host, old authorization/raw local claim/native claim,
failed closure, all old ledgers and exact frozen question/targets/body packet.
The native claim must already exist and remain uncompleted. No replacement claim
or writable-store fallback is available.

The historical reservation remains **77,980 micro-USD**, including the original
36,660 and two expired 20,660 holds. Allow at most **eight** new 20,660 holds,
within **243,260 micro-USD combined**. Every attempted request stays charged
against this retained ceiling, including rejected, failed and unknown outcomes.
These are conservative reservation ceilings, not verified supplier invoices.
Search, source/creator payment, new inbound signing/submission, funding and new
schedules are forbidden. Existing source and custody authority is preserved.

The separate catalogue commits every grant pointer, attempt, hold, normalized
checkpoint, outcome and fixed diagnostic by raw hash. Exclusive fsynced intent
precedes dispatch. A durable execution lock serializes attempts. Crash, partial
publication or uncertain synchronization keeps the lock held; PID age and file
deletion cannot grant automatic takeover. Known completed attempts retain their
closure before releasing only their own lock. Deleting or changing catalogue
entries fails validation rather than resetting the budget.

## Evidence and recovery

Only the same frozen two official sources and all five requested targets are
used. Each attempt has three semantic stages: sufficiency, cited-statement
generation and separate complete evidence review. Generation uses the existing
authorized **8,192 output-token** ceiling instead of the ordinary two-source
2,560 allowance. Ordinary research budgets are unchanged. Input, quote, review,
support and exact original-identity checks are preserved. Unbound, malformed,
truncated or incompletely reviewed output cannot become a prepared delivery.

An exact normalized successful checkpoint may be reused only within the same
grant/source/packet and identical stage/prompt/token bounds. A failed request
gets a new hold if finite allowance remains; its old hold is never reused.
Fixed phase/category diagnostics expose useful failure location while retaining
provider bodies, prompts and credentials privately. They do not retrospectively
identify the old execution's lost exception.

`npm run operator:continue-original -- preflight --authorization <file> --sha256
<digest>` checks the frozen prompts and fresh native claim through read-only
storage. `execute` prepares only. Privately inspect the actual complete answer,
quotes, target coverage and explicit evidence gaps, then use `verify-prepared`
and `complete-prepared --prepared-sha256 <exact-reviewed-file-digest>`.
Completion is idempotent metadata for the same permanent claim, with fresh
native result/settlement proof before publishing a delivered marker. A lost
acknowledgement permits observation and exact metadata recovery, never repayment.

identifier-free closure projection and original authenticated GET response.
Desktop, extensions and bots retain their hosted adapters; no new installer,
client package, scheduler, wallet role or public activation route is introduced.
SQLite native/enrolled completion uses the existing reviewed schema; PostgreSQL
remains unsupported for this domain. No schema migration is introduced here.

Focused adversarial tests, both TypeScript graphs, lint, production build,
independent review and required PR checks gate release. Verify the actual merged

preserve every old journal/custody/build backup. Only actual same-original native
