empty payment-feed response removed the 40px strip between measurement and capture.

beside it, and fits the decorative globe below it inside a 100px header. Desktop
spacing and 140px globe remain unchanged. No text, form control, source-cap

both fictional retained and error feeds at 320/390×640. The 320px regression must
positively wrap the source-cap disclosure into two lines while retaining the
