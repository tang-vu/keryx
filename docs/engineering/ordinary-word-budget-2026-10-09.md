app 0.27.44 answer has 296 whitespace-delimited words, despite retaining all three
requested facts. Preserve that historical failure and its source/version bindings.
The new source candidate addresses renderer overhead, not extraction or model

## Recognition and rendering

From the trusted original caller only, recognize these finite positive English
commands at a clause boundary: `Keep [the] note/answer/response within N words`,
`Keep [the] note/answer/response at most N words`, and `Answer/Respond in at most/no
more than N words`. Optional `Please` is supported. N is an unpadded integer 1–2000.
Quoted/negated commands, ambiguous different limits, fractions, region exclusions
and arbitrary research numbers do not activate this role. Unsupported positive
output-language requests and mixed short-bullet requests retain their old policy.
This does not broaden language inference or add locale/catalogue behavior.

Only ordinary public English runs participate. Retained/private originals,
execution-limited or target/paid-scholarly packages, decision briefs and teaching
mode retain their existing presentation and money policies. The canonical answer
given to attribution remains byte-identical to the answer before this projection;
all contributions, reward weights/amounts and settlements are decided first.

At finish, a compact candidate retains every admitted sentence with its exact
source excerpt and marker, all other qualified excerpts, uncovered/partial target
gaps, the full recorded Low confidence rationale, source-grounding/entailment and
source-conflict limitations, and the separate payment-receipt caveat. Only repeated
introductions and fully covered target/topic labels are removed. Quotes use the
same display escaping as historical grounded delivery; evidence/receipt quote
bytes and flags are unchanged. Neither renderer creates evidence capabilities.

Both original and compact bodies receive the identical supplied-original status,
recency gaps/observations, funding notice and exact post-attribution follow-up guidance. Count whitespace-delimited words in
the entire final answer. Prefer an already-fitting original; select the compact
candidate only when it fits. Otherwise retain the full original plus an explicit
unmet-limit notice. Never truncate reviewed text or drop targets to force a cap.
An early finish has no compact evidence candidate and keeps the same full-content
fallback. Earlier synthesis trace entries retain the canonical pre-attribution
answer; a final trace entry records the measured projection, and done/storage/
receipt/export consumers receive the final answer. No published trace is mutated.
