# Bounded ordinary-browser research acceptance

The owner authorized one new acceptance round on October 5: at most three questions,
USD 1 combined model and search cost, and zero USDC source purchases or creator rewards.
Previous allowances and their retained failures remain closed. This document describes
the prerequisite implementation; it does not claim that the round ran or passed.

## Payment and compute authority

An explicit source budget of zero is preserved through the browser composer, SSE API,
OpenAI-compatible API and remote MCP. The shared agent withholds gateway deliveries
and rewards at zero, including a legacy zero-price delivery that would otherwise enter
the gateway. A connected browser session creates no signing scope for a zero-budget ask.
Free public originals, valid cached reads and creator-authorized free article delivery
retain their normal evidence and attention gates. Model/search operating costs are separate.

The optional, strict `keryx-production-research-allowance-v2` uses the existing paired
`KERYX_MODEL_ALLOWANCE_FILE` and `KERYX_MODEL_ALLOWANCE_SHA256` controls. A protected,
digest-bound policy admits only its three exact question hashes, ordinary public web
origin, Quick mode, treasury provenance and zero source budget. It does not admit a
paid manuscript or private execution. The existing v1 model-only policy and its dated
closed journal are preserved without conversion or renewal.
Question hashes constrain scope, not operator identity: another public web caller with
the exact approved text could consume that one slot. It cannot enlarge the allowance;
the retained browser intent and original dispatch must establish the actual submitted case.

The reviewed October 5 [DeepSeek tariff](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/quick_start/pricing/)
gives Flash peak input USD 0.30/M and output USD 1.20/M; the legacy request alias is billed
as V4.1 Flash. The [Tavily tariff](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/api-credits)
gives one credit per basic search and gross PAYG USD 0.008/credit. Account discounts,
granted credits and actual invoices are unknown. No automatic price/date renewal exists.

The fixed ceiling is 46 model holds of 20660 microUSD plus six basic-search holds of
8000 microUSD: **998360 microUSD**, within USD 1. Prompts remain bounded at 32000 UTF-8
bytes plus the reviewed framing allowance, output at 8192 tokens; no prompt truncation,
alternate provider, retry or refund expands capacity. Search is limited to two attempts
per admitted question. Protected exclusive files and file/directory fsync reserve capacity
before actual transport across processes. Empty, interrupted, failed and unknown attempts
stay consumed across restarts. Changed policy, foreign execution context, malformed journal,
expiry or exhausted capacity refuses additional dispatch.

## Operator acceptance and closure

Keep the prior decision brief disabled. Pause and positively verify the private worker
inactive with PID zero before activating this allowance: its explicit private engine is
outside the public factory. Preserve all paid private jobs, execution claims, custody,
locks and recovery state. Keep prior schedules held; direct model watchdogs are outside
this allowance. Both public roles must use the same reviewed policy/digest. A new scope
and journal require exclusive protected creation; never replace the old closed scope.
New paid A2A/Monthly admission is withheld before settlement or consuming a prepaid slot.
The public worker withholds claims on already-paid queued jobs until ordinary execution
returns. Original GET/status/delivery and existing Monthly request-identity replay remain
available; no queued original is failed or requeued merely because the trial is active.

Submit each frozen question once from a new ordinary browser conversation, after recording
its exclusive intent. Do not use auto-submit links, SDK/direct-engine probes or a historical
answer replay. A failed or unknown question consumes its opportunity. Capture the original
dispatch, saved source budget, extraction/evidence outcomes and durable reservations.
Technical acceptance checks all cumulative caps and zero payment/funding/signing attempts;
independent usefulness checks completeness and support against the frozen criteria.
Service success, target coverage and intact quotations alone do not prove useful synthesis.
Retain failed results and report the actual denominator without replacement spending.

At closure preserve the new policy and journal, restore the reviewed steady public roles,
then restore only the private worker's previous active state. Recheck health, queues, financial
controls and the unchanged original closed journal. Reservations are conservative ceilings,
not supplier invoices or independent customer traction. The separately authorized five-job
mainnet schedule can activate only after its own acceptance and installed-file verification;
this feature does not install a scheduler or authorize payment retries.

## Supported surfaces and release gates

App **0.26.16 candidate** changes shared hosted execution, web/SSE, OpenAI API and remote
MCP **0.3.1** together. Hosted clients, bots and the extension inherit the shared zero-source
payment guard. The bounded trial deliberately admits only ordinary web: remote MCP, bots,
A2A, local CLI and private jobs do not gain trial spending authority. Local processes need
their own explicit operator controls; a policy on the server does not constrain another host.

Caller-funded stdio MCP **0.4.5**, Operator **0.4.6** and extension **0.1.1** retain their
existing custody, positive paid-checkout contracts and browser handoff roles. They gain no
native Google signer, model allowance, scheduler or automatic spending authority. Their
authored package graphs are unchanged; inherited distribution and new hosted deployment
still need exact-source CI, build and public readback. No funded mainnet payment, custody
recovery, security audit or backup restore gate is closed by these changes.
