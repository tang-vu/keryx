# Practical method requests

First-person questions such as “How should we measure the pilot?” or “How can I
estimate costs?” can now request a proposed approach through the existing ordinary
work-product contract. Recognition uses bounded English/Vietnamese action grammar,
independent of the question's subject, source or customer. It is not a complete
semantic intent classifier. Third-person descriptions and dictionary questions do
not acquire this contract.

The whole unchanged caller question remains the criterion, bound by SHA-256.
Existing recognized requests keep precedence. Quotes, source envelopes, prepared
requests, negation, output limits and teaching/private/first-turn gates retain
their authority. The recognized action names an output, never permission to run
steps, contact anyone or spend money.

Proposed sections still require retained factual premises and the existing
independent review of scope, conditions, language and proposal-only wording. A
missing or rejected premise withholds dependent sections. Proposed sections append
after attribution and settlement; they cannot contribute evidence coverage or
creator reward weight. The change adds no model call or retry and does not claim
equal token usage or cost.

Frozen-question recognition and controlled orchestration verify admission and
delivery boundaries. They do not prove historical causation, actual model
usefulness, source truth or outside-user demand. In particular, quoting one
organization's experience does not establish that its method suits another caller;
that remains a factual and proposal-review obligation.

Web/API, desktop, CLI, MCP, extension and bot retain their existing result and
report handoffs. No new input field, storage schema, payment path, feature flag or
distribution version is introduced. Production delivery remains a separate step.
