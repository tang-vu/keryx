# Caller-requested proposed work products

The current history audit exposed a missing output: a month of speaking
preparation was requested, but only qualified source excerpts were delivered.
Other callers requested operational checklists and comparisons. Custom briefs now
request a bounded proposed draft using arbitrary caller deliverables and review
criteria. No subject or IELTS answer is built into the parser. These source
checks do not establish useful live model delivery or guaranteed outcomes.

## Original caller and execution

`prepareSpecialistBrief({ track: "custom", ... })` starts the question with
`Prepare a proposed work product.`. Its existing requested-deliverable and
review-criteria blocks bind a strict request to the SHA-256 of the whole original
question. Duplicate or malformed blocks refuse the addition. All maximum field
inputs still fit the existing 2,000-character question bound, without clipping
caller text or URLs. The other presets and request envelopes remain unchanged.

The structured request keeps its existing version 1 schema. Clear ordinary
requests can also ask directly for a proposed checklist, plan, runbook, brief or
comparison, including first-person preparation questions. Bounded English,
Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese grammar recognizes an output label from the
original caller text. Quoted/code spans, source-attributed instructions, negated
output requests and prepared envelopes cannot select this ordinary format.
Classroom generation keeps its separate gate. Recognition is conservative and
does not claim general intent understanding.

An ordinary version 2 request retains the entire unchanged original question as
its single review criterion, up to the existing 2,000-character public limit.
The label alone cannot discard language, time horizon, URLs, negation, missing
inputs or qualifications. It is metadata rather than a factual premise or a
completion verdict. The original digest, exact parser result and full criterion
must all match; the independent review sees the original question in full.
Existing structured deliverable/criterion limits are unchanged. A longer review
criterion still passes the original input/output and review packet preflight;
refusal keeps the existing factual flow without another attempt.

Only an explicit first-turn public caller can select this format. Added follow-up
context, private jobs, wanted assets, paid scholarly work, decision briefs,
teaching mode and explicit bullet/sentence/word limits retain their prior flows.
The complete previously admitted teaching request also refuses this parser.
Classroom activities, lesson scripts, classification exercises and exit questions
are outside this contract and must be rejected in its independent review; the
separate teaching activation hold remains.

Fresh public package callers can opt in within their accepted execution limits.
Attention, expansion rounds, provisional SLO, best-effort quality, fees and quote
authority remain. This is a format request, never another read, payment or model
attempt. Stored originals and historical results gain no new authority.

## Existing generation and independent review

Generation may add at most six sections with unique IDs, headings up to 80
characters, text up to 600, one to four distinct factual premise IDs, and at most
two hypothetical conditions of 180 characters. `eN` refers to the Nth resolved
evidence row, including unresolved positions. Premises retain statement, exact
quote, target and marker. Duplicate physical premises under different IDs cannot
create extra dependencies. Invalid products are withheld rather than truncated.

A copied, digest-bound packet enters the existing factual review, preserving
every admitted factual row and full source neighborhood. The exact digest,
ordered premise IDs, every section and every caller criterion require complete,
unique judgments. Review separately checks premises, proposal-only wording,
requested scope, conditions and language. Missing/foreign judgments refuse the
whole proposal review; explicit rejection may leave approved siblings.

Hypothetical schedule allocations must preserve the caller horizon and missing
inputs. They establish no measured efficacy or outcome guarantee. New facts,
unsupported comparisons, source-performed claims, teaching activities and
consequential medical/legal/financial recommendations are rejected.

The original supplier preflight, output allowances and compute admission remain.
An addition refused in preflight retains the ordinary factual packet. Review
keeps the 32,000-byte bound and 1,024-byte framing reserve. There is no proposal-only
review, repair or retry. Whole-call compute/output refusal retains existing
failure handling. Prompt size and actual cost can change; equal call counts do
not establish equal cost.

## Final delivery and supported surfaces

The runtime review capability cannot be restored from JSON or mutated to promote
rejected rows. Final delivery rechecks each exact admitted statement, marker,
final support threshold and unique source/item/URL/version. Lost premises withdraw
dependent sections and the overall criteria-completion indication.

The draft, conditions, caller checklist and factual premises are appended only
after canonical attribution and settlement. They add no factual coverage,
confidence, citation weight, creator allocation or execution authority. A draft
over 12,000 characters is withheld completely; facts and qualifications are never
clipped. System labels support English, Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese, with
English fallback otherwise. Proposal text receives caller-language review.

All existing consumers of the final answer inherit the draft: web, desktop,
repository CLI, API, remote/stdio MCP and bot handoffs. Reports and receipt answer
hashes retain it. Internal packets and review capabilities are never serialized
as public result fields. A receipt digest checks data integrity, not semantic
quality or human acceptance. No storage schema, checkout, public authority field,
package/runtime version or automatic deployment is added.

## Verification scope

Controls cover caller binding, tampering, malformed/duplicate reviews, unresolved
row positions, physical identity ambiguity, final premise withdrawal, review
outage, input/output refusal, rendering bounds and protected paths. Actual shared
agent cases use synthetic source text and controlled model/reviewer judgments for
a four-week speaking plan, operations checklist and comparison note. Paired cases
keep the original question fixed and disable only the synthesis addition in the
control; attribution input, evidence, reads, citations and financial result must
remain identical. Rendered answer, report and valid receipt retain the draft
without its private review object. No live model/search/payment allowance or
observed successful live IELTS delivery is claimed.

The same controls also exercise ordinary caller requests without a custom-brief
wrapper. A rejected full-question criterion cannot promote a partial proposal to
completion. Historical public answers motivated these cases; controlled model
responses establish contract behavior rather than successful live task delivery.
