# Ordinary research compute bounds

New ordinary reasoning engines share one request-local allowance across provider
fallbacks and both balanced-routing lanes: 32 supplier calls, 1,048,576 cumulative
UTF-8 bytes in application system/user prompts, and 131,072 cumulative requested
output tokens. These prospective work bounds are separate from source USDC,
creator rewards, package pricing and supplier billing. Prompt content bytes do
not include transport framing; requested output tokens are reservations rather
than measured generation. A dollar margin still requires actual cost evidence.

Admission happens after existing input validation and before the supplier-call
ledger or transport. Synthesis and its separate evidence review each consume a
call. Failed or uncertain attempts keep their allowance. Refused calls consume
nothing and cannot authorize retries, another provider, or circuit failure/success
updates. Existing same-input coverage reuse makes no new supplier call.

Only the ordinary selector binds fresh engines. Direct fixed-model engines,
finite production allowances, business-canary engines and retained private
originals keep their existing independently admitted contracts. New requests do
not change old financial originals, source budgets, signing, reservations,
settlement, rewards, refunds or recovery. An unavailable evidence review continues
to withhold unsupported evidence under the existing delivery contract.

The web, ordinary API/OpenAI-compatible, remote MCP and bot routes inherit the
same shared engine behavior. Desktop, extension and caller-funded stdio roles keep
their existing local/hosted handoffs; there is no new request field, payment
permission or native implementation. Package/desktop publication and deployment
remain part of the existing coordinator's accepted release batch.

Hermetic tests exercise cumulative byte/token/call limits, failed attempts,
cross-provider and balanced sharing, terminal refusal without provider-health
changes, separate evidence review, exact coverage reuse and independent engines.
They call no model, search provider or payment service and establish no current
useful-answer rate or measured vendor savings.
