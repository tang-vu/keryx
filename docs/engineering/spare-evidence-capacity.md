# Reusing covered structural samples

HTML evidence selection keeps the original lexical passages and effective primary
structural context. For complete, observed HTML role maps with a non-ASCII research
target and no explicit heading/fragment/gloss selection, the small neutral body
sample can spend all four opportunities on blocks already wholly delivered by the
lexical result. A later intact body then remains absent even when space is free.

The selector now examines a fixed reserve of four additional body offsets. Covered
primary blocks consume no offered slot or constructed window. Effective original
primary candidates retain precedence, including the original nomination ceiling's
admission order. Only then can reserve blocks fill remaining room. Navigation,
limited or unregistered role maps, preformatted and enumerated bundles keep their
existing exclusions. Each observed section still supplies at most two candidates.

The limits remain four offered uncovered samples, eight candidate offsets, nine
final passages, 2,000 selected characters and the existing shared nomination and
retention ceilings. No selected byte is displaced. This is structural sampling;
it does not establish relevance, completeness or entailment, translate a target,
read another source, increase a model allowance or change confidence/reward policy.

The downstream quote menu is also bounded at 64 choices per source. Dense reserve
text must not crowd out a late original quote. Private, in-process metadata binds
the original selected ranges to the final passage array and unchanged source text.
The menu offers the original sentence and optional short-block/adjacent choices
first, retaining their IDs, then fills spare slots from the extended selection.
Source segmentation is shared between the two phases. All final passages are
validated first. Copied or shortened selections cannot recover removed spans from
the ordering hint. It never supplies eligibility, support or payment authority.

Regression fixtures use genuine HTML extraction and role registration, four target
languages, exact source offsets, effective-primary precedence, tight character and
slot limits, excluded structures, dense quote menus under all four option policies,
and removal of an original selected passage. An empty proposal ledger remains
ineligible regardless of the retrieved context. Existing original NASA teaching,
unsupported-attribution, unavailable-review and confidence controls are retained.
The reproducible paying-source study runs without outbound providers. These checks
establish bounded retrieval behavior; they do not measure live answer usefulness
or show that every historical Low confidence answer has this cause.

The shared evidence/quote path applies to existing web/API and agent consumers;
desktop, CLI, MCP, extension and bot adapters keep their existing roles and public
contracts. No release version, deployment or provider/payment authority changes.
