# Prospective application requests

A caller may ask how to make or adapt an operation without naming a plan or
checklist. Finite English modal-action questions and Vietnamese requests for a
proposed way, procedure or approach can select the existing ordinary version 2
work-product contract. Recognition is subject-independent and bounded by the
existing 2,000-character limit; it is not a complete semantic intent classifier.

Existing recognized labels keep precedence. The request retains the entire
unchanged caller question as its criterion, plus its SHA-256 binding. A recognized
verb supplies only an output label, never a fact, a completed task or permission to
execute a step. Missing facts remain missing. The quoted-source, prepared/private,
first-turn, answer-format and classroom-generation boundaries still apply.

Quoted/code text, including escaped quotes and multi-backtick fences, cannot create
the opt-in. Labeled source headers remain inert across normal and Unicode line
separators. Newly recognized prospective forms reject explicit no-procedure
instructions and protected teaching artifacts; clear Portuguese/Spanish output
counts also prevent an extra proposed draft. This does not implement those word
limits or broaden teaching activation.

The existing generation and independent factual/proposal review add no new call or
retry. Whole caller criteria, exact retained premises, proposal-only wording,
conditions and language remain independently reviewed within the same byte/token
ceilings. A lost or rejected factual premise withholds dependent sections. Drafts
append only after factual attribution and settlement, adding no evidence coverage,
reward weight or execution authority. Equal call counts do not imply equal cost.

Controlled checks use synthetic transport/reviewer responses and compare actual
orchestration, factual/financial outputs, receipt/export and protected-scope
behavior. Frozen-history recognition identifies eligible requests, not historically
completed answers or a measured useful-model success rate. Full study/original and
actual PR/main/native/domain/platform acceptance remain required.
