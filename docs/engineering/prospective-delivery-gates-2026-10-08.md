# Prospective teaching delivery gates - October 8, 2026

Issue [211](https://github.com/tang-vu/keryx/issues/211) requests both sourced facts
and a proposed lesson. Ordinary delivery currently admits quote-bound reviewed
sentences; it has no reviewed proposal contract. The planner now keeps activities,
invented examples, exit questions and presentation constraints out of historical
NASA factual targets. This repairs decomposition without claiming the completed
teaching deliverable exists.

The staged design must preserve these boundaries before activation:

1. Use a typed, false-default deliverable request bound to the original caller and
   its requested language, duration, counts and word limit. Neither source text,
   prior context nor model output can activate it. Establish how ordinary natural
   language enters that contract across the shared research surfaces; a keyword
   classifier or an unthreaded public flag is not a completed integration.
2. Generate bounded proposals with unique IDs and explicit premise IDs referencing
   exact quote-bound statement rows. Preserve ordinary generation and review caps.
   Include separate proposal judgments in the existing independent review packet,
   covering every premise, its conditions, instructional consistency and absence
   of unsupported historical/source-execution claims. A prompt alone is insufficient.
3. After final assessment and ledger construction, every premise must still be an
   admitted, independently model-reviewed cited statement. Missing, duplicate,
   malformed or over-budget dependencies withhold the affected proposal. Never
   repair an invalid packet by inventing a binding or making another supplier call.
4. Render proposals separately and label them as proposed activities/examples.
   Citations attach to the factual premises, not a claim that NASA designed or
   tested the lesson. Proposal rows create no factual coverage, confidence,
   citation-marker or creator-reward authority. Preserve every admitted factual
   excerpt and existing payment state.
5. Validate requested count/word/duration constraints deterministically. If factual
   support or proposal review fails, preserve useful admitted facts and explicitly
   identify the missing teaching output and a targeted factual next step. Preserve
   the completed stream and receipt; do not turn a proposal failure into a paid
   retry or an unsupported recommendation to read a video transcript.

Implementation needs scoped planning/admission and shared result/export adapters,
then adversarial dependency/overflow/partial-review and actual rendered fixtures.
Retained private originals and absent opt-ins must preserve their existing input
and prompt contracts. Only separately authorized bounded lesson acceptance can
establish the requested useful output. This design neither activates a prototype
nor creates supplier, payment, funding or scheduler authority.
