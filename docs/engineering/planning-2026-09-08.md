# Planning scope evaluation, September 8, 2026

The paid pilot generated a third target asking what the Engineering documentation
says, duplicating its two substantive recovery/journaling questions. Version 0.22.11
asks the planner to list source, language and style constraints before producing
substantive research targets. This is internal model output: the unchanged original
question remains available to source selection, decisions, assessment and synthesis.
Source restrictions are not deterministically enforced by this prompt.

## Reproduce

`node --import tsx scripts/eval-planning-scope.mts --check` validates six fixtures
without model requests. Set `KERYX_LLM_MODEL=deepseek-v4-flash` and run
`node --import tsx --env-file=.env.local scripts/eval-planning-scope.mts --live`
for three rounds of six direct model requests. This incurs model API usage, has no
reasoning fallback or payment calls, and saves completed rows under ignored
`.artifacts/evals/`. Semantic results require manual inspection; exit zero does not
assert that all planning expectations passed.

The fixtures cover the paid-pilot question, one fact with format instructions,
explicit source reliability, source disagreement, unrelated mangrove research and
a Vietnamese recovery question. They are small first-party diagnostics, not an
independent benchmark.

## Observations

Two preliminary prompt variants still generated redundant targets or failed to split
the Vietnamese question. In the final 18 outputs, manually inspected:

- The pilot question consistently yielded two substantive targets, with no duplicate
  documentation-summary target (3/3).
- Single-fact prompts produced one timing target (3/3); explicitly requested source
  reliability and source comparison remained substantive (6/6).
- Mangrove prompts preserved salt tolerance and erosion without a language/source-only
  target (3/3).
- Vietnamese prompts retained both requested topics, but one output combined them
  into one question (2/3 separately split). Output language was not consistently Vietnamese.
- Source qualifiers were not consistently repeated in individual targets. They remain
  in the original question supplied to downstream steps; this is a remaining limitation.

A separate model-only pass used the real RSS article bodies and the exact paid-pilot
question through decomposition, sufficiency, synthesis/relevance review and the existing
evidence ledger. It produced two targets with coverage 0.9 each. This single pass did
not purchase content or settle rewards and does not revise the earlier paid job's
0.666667 result. No evidence score or old receipt was edited.
