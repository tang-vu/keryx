# Independent research pilot

Status, September 30, 2026: no independent buyer is committed yet. Recruitment and
paid repeat-use validation remain open. This runbook prepares the pilot; it does
not claim customers, revenue or outreach.

## Initial customer hypothesis

Target founders and small engineering/product teams building APIs, agents or
payments in the Arc/Circle ecosystem. Select teams with a recurring decision:
which SDK/API to adopt, what changed in a payment integration, or how competing
products compare. Validate this narrow hypothesis before expanding the audience.

Creators are independent technical publishers with useful, current material they
own or can license. Enrolling a source and paying it does not establish buyer demand.

## Deliverable

One bounded research brief answers one real decision question. Before starting,
show scope, freshness cutoff, delivery expectations, total price, service fee,
creator budget and the applicable unused-reserve policy. Existing fixed-price
terms retain unused reserve without automatic refund; pilot offers cannot silently
change these terms. Testnet settlement must be visibly labeled and cannot count as
real paid-market validation.

The brief includes the recommendation and alternatives, cited evidence, publication
dates, conflicting claims, uncertainties and visible BUY/SKIP/CACHE/spend decisions.
The buyer can inspect receipts and reopen the result for a follow-up. Failures and
incomplete coverage must be visible. Obtain the buyer's acceptance against their
actual decision, rather than only testing that an answer was generated.

## Acceptance and evidence

| Stage | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Intake | Independent team, recurring question, acceptance rubric and agreed terms. Record minimal identifying data privately. |
| Delivery | Buyer confirms citations support the main claims, material conflicting evidence is disclosed and the brief helps their decision. Record revision requests. |
| Payment | Reconcile actual settlement and fees; distinguish simulations, testnet, owner-funded and independent real paid orders. |
| Return | Buyer independently orders another useful brief; invitations or repeat owner runs are insufficient. |
| Economics | Complete usage and invoice coverage, creator payments, failed jobs/retries, support, acquisition, refunds/losses and fixed costs in private records. |

Initial event cohort target: 2–3 independent teams, 10 real research jobs and at least
2 returning buyers. These are targets, not achieved counts. Long-term E1 acceptance
still requires 3–5 recurring independent buyer teams, independently controlled
sources and four weeks of cohort evidence. Measure both contribution after costs
and retention; a cheap API alone does not demonstrate a sustainable ecosystem.

Keep invoices, operating costs and customer records out of public telemetry and
repository commits. Publish only verified, authorized figures with their network,
period and provenance. See [business model](./business-model.md) and
[mainnet gates](./mainnet-delivery-plan.md).
