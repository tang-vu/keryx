# Sponsored research and operating fees

The public trial needs no wallet or deposit. The composer shows who pays. Active
personal budgets retain their signer; expired/paused budgets do not fall back to
treasury. Existing quota, prefunding, per-query and lifetime caps remain in force.
No new funding, custody or schedule is created.

The first outreach cohort targets ten CS/blockchain graduate students or PhD
candidates preparing related work or a literature review, starting with former
coauthors and labmates. This is a target, not enrolled participants. Public access
is broader than that cohort. Trial reports have public links: use shareable
questions. Literature notes stay in the browser, but submitted questions become
reports. Preparing a question/comparison makes no research or payment request.

## Fees and source claims

On October 7 the founder authorized actual sponsored operating transfers to the
Keryx/founder recipient, displayed separately from creator rewards. An eligible
unclaimed public citation uses its original contribution share of the existing
citation pool for **Keryx operating fees**. Reading the public page remains free.
The fee pays for Keryx's research service; it grants no publisher ownership.

The pool is at most half the query budget. At 0.05 USDC, an all-public eligible
answer allocates at most 0.025 USDC. Access tolls, creator rewards and operating
fees together remain under the original query cap and the same finite treasury
lifetime cap. Model/search costs are separate. No withheld share is redistributed.

The recipient comes from a protected reviewed policy bound to mainnet storage,
origin and expiry. Request JSON and model output cannot choose it. Exact page
claims and known article-to-feed/homepage associations are checked before allocation
and again inside atomic original admission. Retained public reference items and
already-loaded registered items carry the same association across catalog and web
IDs. All exact matches are unioned, including inactive public references. A retained claim/history blocks that share;
unavailable ownership withholds it. No domain-wide ownership is inferred.

A verified creator claim affects future originals, retaining historical payees.
Earning still needs linked fresh registry authority, enabled citation-only/paid
policy, distribution permission and evidence. A free/disabled claim alone is not
payable. Existing eligible creator payments continue to the creator's wallet.

## Settlement and evidence

Fees use x402/Circle and the dedicated public hosted signer with unchanged
prefunded/query/lifetime accounting. `operating-fee` is distinct from creator
`fetch`/`citation`. One query admits at most one operating original, including
pending/failed originals. Lost responses never grant a replacement nonce. Originals
bind the query, amount, beneficiary, policy and allocation digest. The seller
requires the exact submitted hosted original before Circle settlement.

Payment proof separates settled volume, operating fees and creator rewards.
Canonical rows with actual settlement evidence alone count; planned, pending,
failed and simulated values stay separate. Public wording is neutral; original
payer/payee and funding provenance remain inspectable. Gateway references identify
batch settlements, not individual Arc transaction hashes.

SourceRegistry manages source ownership and payout authority; it is not the
per-payment ledger. Hackathon evidence should link the user task, delivered report,
payment receipts and relevant network evidence. Registry calls alone do not show
research use. Product use, settlement and independent willingness to pay are
separate measurements.

## Surfaces and release gates

| Surface | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Web/literature | Sponsored/public-report messaging, manual preparation, visible payer, wait/retry guidance after quota refusal. |
| Public hosted API/CLI/MCP/extensions/bots | Shared pipeline may use the reviewed public treasury fee. Responses and receipts distinguish operating fees. |
| Browser-funded research | Existing source/creator grants retain authority; no service charge is added to those grants. |
| A2A/private/desktop Operator | Existing fixed package fees/reserves remain authoritative; no second operating debit. Receipt/export readers classify fees separately. |

Unsupported roles withhold the fee. Missing/changed policy closes new operating
transfers while existing research and creator gates remain intact. No schema
migration, new source registration, deposit or top-up is needed. Local checks, CI,
exact production delivery, protected policy activation and genuine bounded
settlement are distinct release gates.

For each consented pilot user, record the real question, usefulness/revisions,
whether they return and actual model/search/USDC/support costs privately. Ask what
helped, what was missing and what still needed checking. Publish only authorized
verified evidence. No outreach, participant, repeat use or settlement is claimed
by this runbook.
