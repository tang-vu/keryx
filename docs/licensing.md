# Licensing scope

As of October 22, 2026, the repository owner has chosen to leave the main Keryx
repository without a repository-wide license. Public visibility alone does not
grant an open-source license to use, modify or redistribute the repository as a whole.
Contact [support@keryx.cc](mailto:support@keryx.cc) with questions about permission.

Some components and files carry their own notices. For example,
[`mcp/package.json`](../mcp/package.json) declares MIT, some inherited Circle source
files carry Apache-2.0 headers, and the bundled fonts retain their individual
[Open Font License notices](../app/fonts). These declarations do not establish a
single license for the entire repository. Preserve each applicable notice and
review the actual files and distribution before reusing a component.

The [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) is adapted from Contributor Covenant
and retains its attribution. Dependencies and third-party documents or content
keep their own terms; this page is not a complete third-party license inventory.

Contributors must have permission to submit their material and retain relevant
copyright, attribution and license notices. A pull request does not create a
repository-wide license or change the terms of another author's work.
