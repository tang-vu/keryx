# Getting help

Start with the [README](README.md), [reviewer guide](docs/reviewer-start.md),
[Windows desktop guide](docs/desktop-alpha.md), [MCP guide](mcp/README.md) or
[browser extension guide](docs/browser-extension.md) for your surface.

- **Bugs and feature requests:** search [existing issues](https://github.com/tang-vu/keryx/issues),
  then use the [issue chooser](https://github.com/tang-vu/keryx/issues/new/choose).
- **Account, installation or private support:** email
  [support@keryx.cc](mailto:support@keryx.cc). Include the affected surface, version,
  environment and a redacted description. Do not send wallet keys, seed phrases,
  session tokens or full environment files.
- **Accessibility barriers:** use the [accessibility reporting options](ACCESSIBILITY.md#report-a-barrier).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** follow the [private reporting policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Community conduct:** email the contact in the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md#enforcement).

English and Vietnamese reports are welcome. Maintainer availability varies; these
channels do not promise an immediate response or a paid support agreement.

For a payment or recovery problem, stop repeated purchase attempts and preserve the
original job and journal privately. A timeout or disconnected client does not prove
that payment failed. Share only a redacted description initially; agree on a private
channel before transferring sensitive records. Maintainers will not ask for your
wallet private key or seed phrase.
